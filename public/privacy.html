Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Fares24x7 ("Fares24x7", "we", "us") operates the website and platform at www.fares24x7.com, which connects businesses that need to transport goods ("customers" or "load owners") with truck owners and transporters ("truck owners"). This policy explains what information we collect, why, who we share it with and the choices you have. It is written to comply with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act") and the Information Technology Act, 2000 and its rules.

Fares24x7 is operated by Fares24x7, India. You can reach us at support@fares24x7.com.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: name, email address, phone number, business name, city, account type (customer or truck owner) and password (stored only in encrypted, hashed form by our authentication provider).
  • Business and KYC details: GSTIN, PAN (personal and company), CIN or other registration numbers, legal business name, designation, billing address and PIN code, and copies of supporting documents such as GST certificates, PAN cards, Aadhaar cards and address proofs.
  • Vehicle details (truck owners): registration number, vehicle type, capacity, engine and chassis numbers, locations and routes, and documents such as RC, insurance, fitness and PUC certificates.
  • Driver details (truck owners): driver name, phone number, driving licence number and expiry, base city, photo, licence copies and identity proof that you upload for your drivers. You confirm you have your drivers' permission to share this information with us.
  • Loads and bookings: pickup and delivery locations, goods description, weight, dates, and the requests you post, accept or decline.
  • Support requests: messages, contact details and any information you include when you contact us.

Information from Google

If you choose "Continue with Google", Google shares your name, email address and profile photo with us. We do not receive your Google password or access your Gmail, contacts, Drive or any other Google data.

Information collected automatically

When you use the platform our service providers record technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information and timestamps for security and troubleshooting. We use your browser's local storage only to keep you signed in and remember basic preferences. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and let you sign in.
  • To verify businesses, vehicles and drivers (KYC) and keep the platform trustworthy.
  • To match truck requests with suitable trucks and send you alerts about relevant requests, acceptances, verification results and support replies.
  • To enable a booking: once a truck owner accepts a request, the customer and truck owner can see each other's business and contact details.
  • To provide customer support, prevent fraud and misuse, and enforce our Terms of Service.
  • To comply with legal obligations and respond to lawful requests from authorities.

We process your personal data on the basis of your consent, which you give when you create an account, and for the legitimate uses permitted under the DPDP Act. You may withdraw consent at any time (see section 6); this may mean we can no longer provide the service to you.

3. Who we share it with

  • Other users, only when needed: truck owners see the details of open truck requests (route, goods, weight, date). Business and contact details are shared between a customer and a truck owner only after a booking is accepted.
  • Service providers who host and run the platform for us under contract: Supabase (database, authentication and document storage), Vercel (website hosting) and Google (sign-in). They may process data on servers located outside India, with appropriate safeguards.
  • Authorities when required by law, court order or to protect the rights, safety and property of our users or the public.

We do not sell your personal data and do not share it with advertisers.

4. How we protect it

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Access is controlled so that users can only see their own records; uploaded KYC, vehicle and driver documents are stored privately and can be viewed only by you and authorised Fares24x7 verification staff through short-lived links. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable security practices and procedures to protect your information and will notify you and the authorities of a personal data breach as required by law.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active and for as long as needed to provide the service. After you close your account we delete or anonymise your personal data, except where we must keep certain records (for example booking or tax records) to meet legal obligations or resolve disputes.

6. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can:

  • ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how it is used;
  • ask us to correct, complete or update inaccurate information (most details can be edited directly in your Business profile);
  • ask us to erase your data and close your account;
  • withdraw your consent;
  • nominate another person to exercise these rights on your behalf in case of death or incapacity; and
  • raise a grievance with us and, if unresolved, with the Data Protection Board of India.

To use any of these rights, email support@fares24x7.com from your registered email address. We will respond within the time required by law.

7. Grievance officer

Email: support@fares24x7.com (subject: "Grievance")
We acknowledge grievances within 24 hours and resolve them within the time required by law.

8. Children

Fares24x7 is a business platform for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page with a new "Last updated" date and, for significant changes, notify you by email or in the app.
